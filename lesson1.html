Lesson 1: Introduction to Mathematics

Learn the basic concepts and importance of mathematics.

What is Mathematics?

Mathematics is the study of numbers, quantities, shapes, patterns and relationships. It helps us understand and solve problems in everyday life.

Why is Mathematics Important?

Mathematics is used in science, technology, business, engineering and many other fields. We also use mathematics in everyday activities such as shopping, measuring and managing money.

Basic Mathematical Operations

The four basic operations are:

1. Addition 5 + 3 = 8
2. Subtraction 9 - 4 = 5
3. Multiplication 6 × 3 = 18
4. Division 12 ÷ 4 = 3

Quick Summary

Mathematics helps us understand numbers, patterns and relationships. Learning the basic operations gives us a strong foundation for more advanced mathematical concepts.
