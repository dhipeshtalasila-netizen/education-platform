Lesson 2: Numbers and Operations

Learn about numbers and the four basic mathematical operations.

What are Numbers?

Numbers are mathematical symbols used to represent quantities. We use numbers every day to count, measure, compare and calculate.

Types of Numbers

Some common types of numbers include natural numbers, whole numbers, integers, fractions and decimals.

Examples Natural numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Whole numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Addition

Addition means combining two or more numbers to find their total.

Example 7 + 5 = 12

Subtraction

Subtraction means taking one number away from another number.

Example 15 - 6 = 9

Multiplication

Multiplication is a repeated addition of the same number.

Example 4 × 5 = 20

Division

Division means splitting a number into equal groups.

Example 20 ÷ 5 = 4

Quick Summary

Numbers help us represent quantities. The four basic operations are addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. These operations are important for solving mathematical problems in everyday life.
